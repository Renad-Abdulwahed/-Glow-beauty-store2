About Our Project ✨

Glow Beauty Store is a web-based platform designed to manage beauty and fashion products such as makeup, perfumes, accessories, and beauty bags. The website allows users to add, view, search, update, and delete products through a simple, elegant, and user-friendly interface.

All product information is dynamically managed and stored using the browser's localStorage API, which allows data to remain persistent and available without the need for a complex backend database. This makes it an ideal, lightweight system for beauty store owners or employees to organize inventory effortlessly, save time, and improve overall management.
